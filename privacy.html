Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

"A Curious Past Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the A Curious Past channels to upload their own videos to YouTube and other platforms through official APIs.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the tool uses Google OAuth to upload videos and read basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm the correct channel. It does not access any other user's data.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
